Pillar 01 / Verification engine

Confidence starts at the source.

NECore's verification engine is designed to orchestrate identity-source lookups, document authenticity checks and specialist biometric services through authorized providers.

Source verification — NIN/BVN verification where legally authorized and commercially available.
Document processing — OCR, document classification, authenticity evidence and exception handling.
Biometric assurance — Licensed liveness and face-match services, with a human-review path.

Government identity sources and specialist biometric checks require external approvals and provider agreements. Availability must be confirmed before production use.

Pillar 02 / Intelligence layer

Risk doesn't stop after onboarding.

NECore's intelligence layer is designed to link identity signals over time, flag suspicious patterns, and support risk-tier changes for permitted monitoring use cases.

Continuous scoring — contextual risk tiers and policies, subject to validated models.
Behavioral anomalies — unusual velocity, device and interaction patterns.
Cross-platform intelligence — anonymized, lawful signals without unnecessary data disclosure.
Pillar 03 / Compliance infrastructure

Make every decision explainable.

Audit events, structured cases and controlled reporting create a traceable path from a verification request to the rationale behind a decision.

Policy rules — configurable identity and risk decisions with version history.
Human review — exceptions, reason codes and independent approvals.
Evidence exports — redacted reports and auditable event history.

Phase 2 / Future capability

An identity the customer can carry.

The approved roadmap introduces portable, digitally signed credentials that users can hold and selectively share. Institutions verify the signed claims without repeatedly receiving raw identity numbers.

  • ✓User-controlled disclosure and sharing duration.
  • ✓Standard public-key cryptography and signature verification.
  • ✓No public blockchain or smart contracts required.
