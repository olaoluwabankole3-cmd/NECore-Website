Our mission

Make trusted identity decisions clearer and more accountable.

NECore's approved product vision joins three fundamental capabilities: the ability to verify who someone is, understand how identity-associated risk changes, and preserve evidence that regulatory and operational teams can trust.

The initial focus is the Nigerian financial-services ecosystem, with a modular architecture intended to support expansion into additional markets and identity contexts.
