Use cases

Built around institutional work.

These institutional workflows illustrate how case management, risk signals and independent review can work together. Production identity checks depend on authorized source access.

Banking and digital banks

Bring individual and corporate onboarding into structured KYC/KYB cases. Track beneficial owners, directors, signatories and evidence; the institution retains the account-opening decision.

Payments and fintech

Create verification cases through a predictable API, retrieve status and route unresolved outcomes for review. Live source checks require approved access.

Compliance and risk teams

Track exceptions, independent review proposals, reason codes and case audit history. Regulatory policy templates are a future capability.

Digital platforms

Coordinate purpose-led verification, applicant consent and case status through reusable institutional workflows.

Fraud investigations

Record permitted anomaly signals, review changes in risk tier and preserve case history. Current scoring is illustrative, not validated fraud detection.

Customer identity experiences

Offer clear consent, status updates and, in Phase 2, controlled sharing through signed credentials.

Institutional onboarding

From evaluation to authorized use.

Each organization should agree the workflow, controls and evidence requirements before real identity data is processed.

  1. Define the use case

    Choose individual KYC, business KYB, risk review or compliance operations.

  2. Evaluate with synthetic cases

    Test the case lifecycle, reviewer roles and evidence outputs without real personal data.

  3. Validate institutional requirements

    Confirm provider entitlements, lawful processing, security controls and applicable sector obligations.

  4. Agree the controlled pilot

    Establish acceptance criteria and operational responsibilities before any authorized live checks.
