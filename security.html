Security & trust

Security should be visible in the architecture.

NECore is designed around purpose-limited verification, clear accountability, minimal exposure of identity data, and independent review for consequential decisions.

Security by design

Trust requires more than a badge.

These are design commitments for the platform. Certifications, formal audits and regulatory approvals will only be published after independent confirmation.

Protection of sensitive data

Encrypted storage and transport, masked attributes, retention controls and strict production access boundaries.

Responsible verification

Never mistake uncertainty for approval.

A temporary provider outage, inconclusive biometric outcome, or conflicting data should produce an explicit state—not a confident but ungrounded decision.

  • ✓Inconclusive and high-risk cases remain reviewable.
  • ✓Consequential decisions can require independent approval.
  • ✓Reviewers can see the evidence and policy behind a conclusion.

Privacy & accountability

A clear model for sensitive identity information.

Purpose limitation

Collect and process only information necessary for a valid verification purpose, with a documented legal basis.

Data minimization

Prefer masked claims and provider outcomes over unnecessary retransmission of raw identity numbers.

Retention & rights

Design clear retention, deletion and data-subject request workflows subject to lawful recordkeeping duties.
