Website privacy notice

How personal information may be handled when visiting the NECore public website.

Draft for client and compliance review. Not an approved policy or contract. Do not activate data collection until the required details are confirmed.

1. Scope and controller

This draft concerns the public marketing website, not production identity verification or biometric processing. The registered controller name, registered address and verified privacy contact must be confirmed before publication.

2. Information and purposes

Website infrastructure may process IP addresses, browser and device information, request times and technical security logs to deliver and protect the website. The site loads Inter fonts from Google's font service, which may receive network request information. Online enquiry collection is currently disabled. If enabled, it is intended to collect name, work email, organization, role, interest and message for business enquiries. Do not submit NIN, BVN, identity documents or biometric information.

3. Lawful basis

Before activation, NECore must document the applicable lawful basis for each activity under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, including website delivery, security and business enquiry handling. Optional processing requiring consent must be separately assessed.

4. Recipients and international transfers

Hosting and font delivery providers may process technical information. Email delivery is not active; if introduced, the provider and relevant recipients must be disclosed. Confirm processor agreements, data locations and lawful transfer safeguards before approval.

5. Retention and security

Define and approve retention periods or criteria for technical logs and future enquiries, along with access restrictions and deletion procedures. No certification or guarantee of absolute security is made.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or erase personal data, restrict or object to processing, request portability where applicable, withdraw consent where relied upon, and challenge qualifying automated decisions. You may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. The approved NECore privacy request channel must be confirmed before publication.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The final notice must reflect a verified inventory of browser storage, cookies, external services and any optional analytics. No claim that all tracking is absent is made.

8. Changes

Publish an effective date and updated notice after review, and communicate material changes as required.
