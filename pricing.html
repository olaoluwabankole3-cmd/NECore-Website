Pricing questions

Does NECore have a public per-verification price?

Not yet. Provider pricing, data-source entitlements, hosting, volume and compliance requirements must be validated before a reliable commercial rate can be published.

Can we evaluate the experience before production?

The proposed platform includes a synthetic-data sandbox and demo flows. Live identity checks require authorized external providers and approved access.

Can we request a tailored commercial proposal?

Yes, once NECore's team has confirmed the use case, integration requirements and available services.
